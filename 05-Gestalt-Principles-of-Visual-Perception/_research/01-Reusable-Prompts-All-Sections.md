**Section 1: Proximity and Similarity — Grouping Forces**

Task: Spacing and styling grouping related elements without boxes, borders, or extra ink.

Absorbed skills:
- Group with space and style
- remove containers
- test grouping

**Section 2: Continuity and Closure — Eyes Complete**

Task: Implied lines and shapes letting viewers complete forms designers only suggest.

Absorbed skills:
- Imply lines and shapes
- reduce drawn elements
- verify completion


---

**Section 3: Figure-Ground — Separation Clarity**

Task: Contrast and layering separating data figures from supporting grounds unambiguously.

Absorbed skills:
- Separate figure from ground
- layer contrast
- remove ambiguity


---

**Section 4: Common Region — Containers that Think**

Task: Cards and panels used sparingly where grouping forces alone cannot carry structure.

Absorbed skills:
- Contain only where needed
- design quiet regions
- justify every box


---

**Section 5: Pragnanz — Simplicity Wins**

Task: Reduction and symmetry disciplines achieving the simplest sufficient visual form.

Absorbed skills:
- Reduce to simplest form
- symmetrize deliberately
- stop adding

**Section 6: Ink Discipline — Data-Ink Ratios**

Task: Chartjunk removal, gridline minimalism, and maximizing data-ink in every graphic.

Absorbed skills:
- Maximize data-ink
- strip chartjunk
- defend every pixel

**Section 7: Testing Perception — Five-Second Reads**

Absorbed skills:

- iterate to instant clarity
