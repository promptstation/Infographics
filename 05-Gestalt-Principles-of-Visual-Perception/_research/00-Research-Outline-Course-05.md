**Section 1: Proximity and Similarity — Grouping Forces**

**Summary:** Spacing and styling grouping related elements without boxes, borders, or extra ink.

**Absorbed Skill:** Group with space and style; remove containers; test grouping.

**Section 2: Continuity and Closure — Eyes Complete**

**Summary:** Implied lines and shapes letting viewers complete forms designers only suggest.

**Absorbed Skill:** Imply lines and shapes; reduce drawn elements; verify completion.

**Section 3: Figure-Ground — Separation Clarity**

**Summary:** Contrast and layering separating data figures from supporting grounds unambiguously.

**Absorbed Skill:** Separate figure from ground; layer contrast; remove ambiguity.

**Section 4: Common Region — Containers that Think**

**Summary:** Cards and panels used sparingly where grouping forces alone cannot carry structure.

**Absorbed Skill:** Contain only where needed; design quiet regions; justify every box.

**Section 5: Pragnanz — Simplicity Wins**

**Summary:** Reduction and symmetry disciplines achieving the simplest sufficient visual form.

**Absorbed Skill:** Reduce to simplest form; symmetrize deliberately; stop adding.

**Section 6: Ink Discipline — Data-Ink Ratios**

**Summary:** Chartjunk removal, gridline minimalism, and maximizing data-ink in every graphic.

**Absorbed Skill:** Maximize data-ink; strip chartjunk; defend every pixel.

**Section 7: Testing Perception — Five-Second Reads**

**Summary:** Glance tests validating that minimal designs communicate instantly and correctly.

**Absorbed Skill:** Run glance tests; diagnose misreads; iterate to instant clarity.

**Section 8: Capstone — Minimal-Ink Graphic**

**Summary:** Full capstone: complex topic communicated with least ink and proven five-second clarity.

**Absorbed Skill:** Deliver minimal-ink graphics; prove instant clarity; document reductions.
