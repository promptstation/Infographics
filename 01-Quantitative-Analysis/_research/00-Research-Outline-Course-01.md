**Section 1: Dataset Orientation — Profiling at Scale**

**Summary:** Shape, types, and quality profiling giving analysts honest first contact with massive datasets.

**Absorbed Skill:** Profile datasets fast; assess quality; scope analysis honestly.

**Section 2: Descriptive Statistics — Honest Summaries**

**Summary:** Distributions, centers, spreads, and outlier-aware summaries that describe without distorting.

**Absorbed Skill:** Summarize distributions honestly; handle outliers; choose fair aggregates.

**Section 3: Anomaly Detection — True Signals**

**Summary:** Statistical tests separating true anomalies from noise, with false-positive discipline.

**Absorbed Skill:** Test anomalies rigorously; control false positives; verify with context.

**Section 4: Correlation vs Causation — Claim Discipline**

**Summary:** Confounder awareness and language discipline keeping visual claims causally honest.

**Absorbed Skill:** Separate correlation from cause; qualify claims; flag confounders.

**Section 5: Segmentation — Finding the Story Groups**

**Summary:** Cohorts and clusters revealing the subgroups where narratives actually live.

**Absorbed Skill:** Segment meaningfully; validate clusters; compare cohorts fairly.

**Section 6: Narrative Mining — Thesis in Numbers**

**Summary:** Insight ranking by importance and novelty, driving toward one defensible core thesis.

**Absorbed Skill:** Rank insights; converge on theses; kill weak narratives.

**Section 7: Uncertainty — Confidence and Limits**

**Summary:** Error bars, sample caveats, and margin honesty shown, not hidden, in final graphics.

**Absorbed Skill:** Quantify uncertainty; caveat samples; display limits visibly.

**Section 8: Capstone — Data Thesis Brief**

**Summary:** Full capstone: massive dataset reduced to a defensible, uncertainty-honest narrative thesis.

**Absorbed Skill:** Deliver data theses; evidence every claim; disclose limits.
