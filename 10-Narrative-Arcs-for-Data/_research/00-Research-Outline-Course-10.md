**Section 1: Thesis-First — Know the Ending**

**Summary:** Takeaway definition before design, ensuring every element serves one complete thesis.

**Absorbed Skill:** Define theses first; align elements; reject drift.

**Section 2: Three-Beat Data Stories — Setup, Turn, Resolve**

**Summary:** Arc mapping giving data stories beginnings that hook, middles that complicate, ends that land.

**Absorbed Skill:** Map three-beat arcs; complicate middles; land endings.

**Section 3: Static Arcs — Flow on One Canvas**

**Summary:** Numbered paths and visual anchors guiding single-canvas narratives start to finish.

**Absorbed Skill:** Path static narratives; anchor flow; guide completely.

**Section 4: Interactive Arcs — Scrollytelling**

**Summary:** Scroll beats and state changes pacing interactive reveals without losing readers.

**Absorbed Skill:** Beat scrollytelling; manage state changes; retain scrollers.

**Section 5: Tension in Data — Questions and Reveals**

**Summary:** Curiosity gaps and paced reveals keeping viewers engaged through complexity.

**Absorbed Skill:** Open curiosity gaps; pace reveals; sustain engagement.

**Section 6: Endings — Takeaway and Action**

**Summary:** Conclusions, calls to action, and source transparency completing the thesis contract.

**Absorbed Skill:** Land takeaways; call to action; source transparently.

**Section 7: Integrity — Arcs Without Distortion**

**Summary:** Honest framing, visible uncertainty, and cherry-picking defenses in narrative graphics.

**Absorbed Skill:** Frame honestly; show uncertainty; resist cherry-picking.

**Section 8: Capstone — Complete Data Thesis**

**Summary:** Full capstone: beginning-to-end data narrative, static and interactive, with integrity proof.

**Absorbed Skill:** Deliver complete data theses; prove integrity; satisfy viewers.
